# Workflow — Explore a Design Direction

**Goal:** produce one or more *competing* art directions to compare in the Design Lab, not a single
safe average (`docs/14`). Use when the direction is genuinely open.

**Do not build production. Do not merge directions into mush — choose one strong direction.**

## Steps
1. **Load context:** `.ai/context/*` + `docs/design-lab/03,04,05,12`.
2. **Frame the tension.** State 2–3 distinct hypotheses (e.g. seed A/B in `docs/14`:
   *Industrial-minimal* vs *Cinematic car-culture*). Each gets a one-line thesis.
3. For **each** direction, run the `art-direction` skill to produce a composition plan (register,
   focal point, type, imagery+provenance, color/light, space, motion). Keep them genuinely different.
4. **Build lightweight** static Lab studies of each (no production polish) — enough to render.
5. **Render + screenshot** (`visual-qa`) across desktop/tablet/mobile.
6. **Critique in parallel personas:** Creative Director, Art Director, Product Designer, Red Team.
   Capture where they **disagree** (that's signal).
7. **Score** each direction: distinctiveness · brand-fit · story · usability · feasibility · perf risk.
8. **Decide:** pick ONE to advance (or a clear hybrid *only* if justified). Record a decision in
   `.ai/decisions/` (hypothesis, options, why, tradeoffs, status) and document strengths/weaknesses of
   each per `docs/14`/QA §36.

## Output
A chosen direction with a named thesis, a decision record, and per-direction critique notes. **Stop**
and get approval before building it out.
