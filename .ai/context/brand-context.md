# Context — Brand (must-load)

Concise routing summary. **Authoritative source:** `docs/design-lab/01-BRAND-CONTEXT.md`.

## The essentials
- **Business:** After Hourz — custom auto body, custom paint, restoration, craftsmanship.
- **Owner:** Anthony Montoya. **Roots:** Southern California.
- **Vehicles:** cars, trucks, motorcycles/bikes.

## Hard guardrails (never drift)
- **NO NorCal branding.** No Northern-California geography/imagery, no "QUINCY" mural, no
  California-outline "NorCal" badge. Legacy poster = **reference only** (`docs/02`, asset A-001).
- SoCal culture may inform tone **authentically** — never as a stereotyped theme.
- Do **not** mutate the brand into: cyberpunk · generic luxury · streetwear · racing/motorsports ·
  futuristic EV · generic mechanic shop. (Only if the client explicitly directs otherwise.)

## Voice / personality
Precise, patient, obsessive about finish · confident and understated · culturally grounded ·
craft-first ("show the work"). **Not** loud, cheap, gimmicky, salesy, or retro-kitsch.

## Thematic language (the vocabulary to stay inside)
after hours · craft · paint · restoration · transformation · metal · chrome · reflection · gloss ·
depth · darkness · controlled light · precision · authentic automotive culture.

## Truth constraint
Never fabricate: address · hours · phone · pricing · years in business · certifications ·
testimonials · customer names · awards · project details · guarantees · social handles · stats.
Unknown ⇒ mark **`CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED`**.
