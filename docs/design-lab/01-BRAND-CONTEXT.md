# 01 — Brand Context

## The business

**After Hourz** — custom automotive body work, custom paint, and restoration.
Owner: **Anthony Montoya.**

Core craft:
- auto body repair & fabrication
- custom paint
- restoration of classic / custom vehicles
- custom vehicle craftsmanship

Vehicle categories: **cars, trucks, motorcycles/bikes.**

## Geography & culture

Anthony is **Southern California raised.** Southern California automotive culture — lowriders,
classic American iron, custom paint, car-club community — may *authentically inform* the tone.

It must **not** become a stereotyped "SoCal" theme (palm trees, sunsets, beach clichés). The
brand should feel grounded, crafted, and specific to the actual work, not to a postcard.

### ⚠️ NorCal is out of scope

The supplied legacy promo poster carries **"NorCal"** branding, a Northern-California map badge,
and a "QUINCY" mural. **None of this carries forward.** Do not use:
- the word NorCal, or any Northern-California geography/imagery
- the California-outline "NorCal" badge
- the "QUINCY" signage
- any regional identity tied to Northern California

The poster is useful **only** as a record of the existing visual vocabulary (chrome lettering,
electric blue, black night-shop atmosphere, lowrider subject matter). We keep the *materials
and mood*, not the *geography*.

## Brand personality

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Precise, patient, obsessive about finish | Loud, cheap, gimmicky |
| Confident and understated | Bragging or salesy |
| Culturally grounded (real car culture) | Stereotyped / theme-park "SoCal" |
| Craft-first ("show the work") | Spec-sheet or franchise mechanic energy |
| Contemporary and considered | Retro-kitsch, flames-and-checkerboard |

## What "premium" means here

Not luxury-black-and-gold. Not spectacle. Premium = **evidence of craft**: the depth of a paint
job, the trueness of a body line, the honesty of a before/after. The digital experience earns
"premium" the same way a paint booth does — through surface quality and restraint, not ornament.

## Explicitly out of scope (visual)

flames · checkerboard · cartoon cars · generic carbon-fiber backgrounds · cheesy neon ·
excessive gradients · gamer aesthetics · luxury black-and-gold · heavy glassmorphism ·
meaningless giant type · random 3D objects · animation for its own sake · tattoo/blackletter
script as a *system* (allowed only as a small, deliberate nod — see doc 06).
