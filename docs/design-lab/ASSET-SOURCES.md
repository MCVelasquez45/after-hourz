## Rejected note

Seven downloaded assets were rejected after rendering (detailed in the Rejected table above). The most
critical is a **metadata/pixel mismatch**: `booth-process/primer-spray-masked-fender-booth-Xbgu6L9YyAE.jpg`
is not a booth primer shot at all — it is the **Cadillac Ranch graffiti installation** (buried,
spray-bombed cars in a desert): off-brand chaos with zero craft, hard reject. The remaining rejects
fall into two groups: (1) one show-bike with garish RGB underglow and heart-motif airbrush that
violates the premium-restraint guardrail, and (2) five environment frames reading as generic
cyberpunk/urban-city atmosphere in a cold teal-magenta register, with no custom-car craft — wrong for
SoCal after-hours culture — plus one beach-rental storefront already flagged `heroSuitable:false`.

Additionally, a large upstream candidate pool was **rejected before download or during evaluation** and
never entered the repo — including NorCal/SF-geography frames (e.g. a flamed Impala tagged
"HYDE ST PIER" — Northern California, forbidden per the regional rule), off-direction JDM/Euro/
motorsports vehicles, flat-daylight documentary snapshots, blown-out/hazy grades, neon-cliche and
streetwear/branded-signage scenes, CGI renders, wrong-subject clusters (freight-train graffiti,
house-painting, barber clippers), and numerous HTTP 403 rate-limited download failures. Those did not
produce local files and so are not tabled here.
