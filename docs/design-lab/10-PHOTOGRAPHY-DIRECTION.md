# 10 — Photography Direction

Photography is the single highest-leverage asset investment (doc 02 gaps). "Booth Light" lives or
dies on imagery. The goal is **cinematic, not documentary** — but always **truthful** (doc 04 P8).

## The truth constraint (read first)

  before/afters. No borrowed shop imagery.
- The legacy poster (A-001) is a *generated composite* — **never** presented as a real photo of his
  work.
- Until real photography exists, we design with the two genuine vehicle photos (A-002/A-003) and

## Look & feel

Cinematic, dark, controlled. Think automotive editorial + industrial product photography under one
key light — the booth. Deep shadows, tight specular highlights on paint and chrome, rich blacks,
restrained color with the electric-blue as an occasional lit accent (doc 07).

**Vehicles (finished):**

## Composition standards

- **Strong negative space** deliberately left for typography (doc 05 cinematic-band, doc 08 grid).
- **Consistent single key light** across a set so the site feels art-directed, not a mixed album.
- Low angles and tight crops for drama; wide clean frames for calm.
- Consistent **aspect ratios** from doc 08 (21:9, 16:9, 3:2, 4:5, 1:1) so crops are intentional and
  layout-shift-free.
- Backgrounds simplified — the busy show background of A-002/A-003 is the kind of thing to avoid or
  control in future shoots (crop, darken, or reshoot on clean ground).

## Working with the current low-res originals

- A-002 (640×459): small render only — gallery card / detail crop. Not full-bleed.
- A-003 (1536×535): usable as a wide band if cropped tight to the body line and the surround is
  darkened/treated (derivative in `derived/treatments/`, original untouched — doc 22/PROVENANCE).
- All treatments are **derivatives**; originals remain immutable.

## Treatment & grading (applied to derivatives only)

- Lift blacks *just* off pure black (match `ink`/`graphite`, doc 07) to preserve shadow detail.
- Protect highlight rolloff on chrome — keep specular crisp, not blown.
- Subtle cool grade tolerated to sit in the palette; **never** recolor the actual paint
  dishonestly (a blue car stays its true blue).
- Add fine grain (doc 05) to stop banding on dark gradients.
- Export AVIF/WebP + responsive `srcset` (doc 15 / QA §22) — via the derivative pipeline, logged in
  PROVENANCE.

placeholder blocks (labeled) for missing shots. This keeps compositions truthful and makes the
photography gap visibly obvious to the client — which is the point.
