# 12 — Reference Study

We study world-class work to extract **principles**, never to imitate (QA §27). The rule:

> ❌ "Make it look like Apple." ✅ "Use long stretches of restraint so one product image can
> command attention without competing UI."

For each reference: **what works · what does NOT belong in After Hourz · principle extracted.**

---

## Apple
- **Works:** disciplined hierarchy; extraordinary product photography given room to breathe;
  controlled typography; scroll *sequencing* that reveals a product beat by beat; deep restraint.
- **Doesn't belong:** their bright/white marketing gloss; mass-consumer polish; feature-grid density.
- **Principle:** *One hero subject per view, surrounded by quiet. Let imagery carry meaning; reveal
  it in sequence, not all at once.* → drives our reveal-from-black + editorial-quiet register.

## Vercel
- **Works:** precision and system consistency; confident technical typography; crisp dark UI;
  responsive clarity; hairline/geometry rigor.
- **Doesn't belong:** developer-product coldness; heavy gradient/"AI" aesthetics; abstract 3D blobs.
- **Principle:** *A tight, consistent system reads as competence.* → drives our token discipline,
  hairlines, spec-plate rigor (doc 05/08).

## SpaceX
- **Works:** cinematic scale; powerful full-bleed photography/video; minimal UI interference over
  imagery; dramatic dark contrast; strong sequencing of big moments.
- **Doesn't belong:** aerospace bombast; sparse content model that assumes world-famous subject.
- **Principle:** *Cinematic imagery + near-zero UI over it = gravity.* → drives cinematic-band
  register and "get the interface out of the image's way" (doc 05).

## Stripe
- **Works:** immaculate information hierarchy; elegant handling of dense content; purposeful color
  accents; subtle, meaningful motion; world-class docs/detail.
- **Doesn't belong:** fintech gradient identity; product-tour density; illustrative style.
- **Principle:** *Even dense content can feel calm with strict hierarchy and one accent color used
  sparingly.* → validates single-accent discipline (doc 07) and technical-index register.

## Linear
- **Works:** opinionated minimalism; superb dark theme with real depth; fast, precise micro-motion;
  keyboard-first; restraint as brand.
- **Doesn't belong:** SaaS-app chrome; purple brand gradient; product-UI patterns.
- **Principle:** *Dark done right = value depth + precise micro-motion, not black + glow.* → directly
  informs the near-black value ladder (doc 07) and mechanical micro-timing (doc 09).

## Porsche (and premium automotive editorial)
- **Works:** reverence for the object; low cinematic angles; restrained luxury (no gold, no shout);
  technical spec typography that feels engineered; material-true color.
- **Doesn't belong:** heritage-luxury coldness; configurator complexity; corporate polish that would
  feel inauthentic for an independent craftsman shop.
- **Principle:** *Respect the machine: cinematic angles + engineered typography + honest material.*
  → our closest spiritual reference; drives photography (doc 10) and spec-plate type (doc 06).

## Premium creative-dev studios (Active Theory, Locomotive, etc.)
- **Works:** bespoke motion/WebGL as *story*; craft in transitions; memorable, owned interactions.
- **Doesn't belong:** effect-for-effect showreels; heavy load; accessibility/perf as afterthoughts;
  motion that fights the user.
- **Principle:** *Bespoke interaction is powerful only when it serves the story and stays performant
  and accessible.* → our 3D/motion "earn it" gates (doc 09/11, QA §26).

---

## The synthesis (what After Hourz takes from all of them)

1. **Restraint + one hero subject per view** (Apple, SpaceX).
2. **A tight, consistent, precise system** (Vercel, Stripe).
3. **Dark with real value depth + precise micro-motion** (Linear).
4. **Reverence for the machine; engineered typography; honest material** (Porsche).
5. **Bespoke interaction only when it serves the story and stays fast/accessible** (studios).

## The anti-pattern to avoid (stated plainly)

"Three.js + gradients + giant type = premium." It isn't. Premium here = **restraint + craft +
material + precision**, verified with motion off (doc 04 P7). The Red Team (doc 14) exists to catch
us importing a trend instead of a principle.
